Notes: double-click (or long-press) anywhere on the timeline, type your text (Shift+Enter for another line), Enter: fr0gOS glyphs it on the goggles at that moment, one line after the next. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag the strip underneath to move along the song. Click the waveform to jump there. Drag a marker to move it. Keys while playing: D drop, B big drop, C chorus, V event, T text, E outro, P phase, N note, Space play/pause, Delete removes the selected cue.

Goggle preview

What fr0gOS will put on the HDZero screen at the playhead, everything centred. Row 1, the cue line: your events and phases. Under it: your text notes. Along the bottom, like a caption: the music: the song title and artist as it starts, DROP IN 3 2 1 and the drop, chorus, outro.

Events (V): a box pops up where you pressed it: type your own word (up to 12 letters), pick the count, Enter. CLIMB with 3 shows CLIMB IN 3 2 1, then CLIMB flashes and goes.

Phases (P) have their own fixed line, top centre, every time: the count-in (EXIT TREE IN 5 4 3 2 1) takes the line, then the new phase name flashes in and stays up until the next phase's count-in, the outro, or the switch going off. Press P (or the button) and a box pops up on the timeline right there: type the phase name and the count-in words, pick the count, Enter. It starts filled in with the next phase in your plan (tree dance, orbits, climb, free fall, catch back to tree dance), so Enter alone takes it. You can still change any of it in the cue list. The count-in uses the count-in tick above: set it to 1 second for a plain 5-4-3-2-1.

3 · Send it

Song title Artist Radio file name
Radio (EdgeTX, Pocket): one switch starts the song and the cues together
  1. Unzip the radio pack onto the radio's SD card (it lands in SOUNDS/en/).
  2. Model → Mixes → CH9 (AUX 5): source = the switch you want for SONG.
  3. Model → Special Functions: that switch → Play Track → your song → 1x.
  4. fr0gOS reads AUX 5 as SONG (mode USER1). Flip the switch: the music starts and the cue clock starts with it. Flip it back to stop the cues.
