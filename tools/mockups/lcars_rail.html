LCARS Side Rails

LCARS down both edges, telemetry in the black letterbox bars top and bottom, video clean in the middle. Two takes for Four Dash Two — animated the way a real Okudagram moves: the rails power on, a light shimmers down them while live, a segment pulses when the pack sags, and both rails cascade to red on a crash. Use the controls to drive it.

All of this works on the goggle. The FC redraws the OSD every frame, so these are character-grid moves, not video effects: the side rails are columns of block glyphs, the telemetry is ordinary text in the black bars; the power-on is segments printing top-down, the shimmer is one brighter block sliding down a rail, the low-batt pulse and red alert are colour-map swaps (glyph → its red copy), the combo bar is a column of blocks growing by one. The rounded caps read as stepped blocks on the grid — the sweep is the feel, not the exact curve.
