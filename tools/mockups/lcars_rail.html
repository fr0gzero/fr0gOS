LCARS Rail Frame

The frame that makes it LCARS — the elbow sweep, segmented rails, the mascot nested in its own badge. Two takes for Four Dash Two, animated the way a real Okudagram behaves: it powers on, it shimmers while live, it pulses when the battery sags, and the whole board goes to red alert on a crash. Hit the controls to see it move.

All of this is doable on the goggle. The FC redraws the OSD every frame, so these are character-grid moves, not video effects: the power-on draw is segments printing cell-by-cell; the shimmer is one brighter block travelling the top rail; the low-batt pulse and red alert are colour-map flips (swap the glyph to its red copy); the combo bar is a row of block glyphs growing by one. The curves read as stepped blocks on the grid — the sweep is the feel, not the geometry.
